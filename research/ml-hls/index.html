Machine-learning-guided compiler optimization for FPGAs

Learning LLVM pass sequences for high-level synthesis

High-level synthesis compiles C/C++ code into FPGA hardware, but LLVM’s optimization pipeline is tuned for CPUs and serves FPGA back ends poorly. With the CAAD Lab and Red Hat, I worked on letting a learned agent choose the LLVM pass sequence instead, rewarded by the quality of the resulting hardware, and compared supervised and reinforcement-learning approaches using RNN, Transformer, and GNN models of the program.
